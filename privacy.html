Privacy Policy — HomeKeeper

Last updated: November 24, 2026

HomeKeeper is made by Bethel Ventures. This policy explains, in plain words, what the app does with your information.

The short version: nothing leaves your phone. HomeKeeper has no servers, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. We do not collect, sell, share, or send your personal information anywhere — the app collects no personal data at all.

How the app works

  • You keep a record of your house right on your phone: a house profile, a systems and appliances inventory with ages and warranty dates, a maintenance schedule, a records vault, a service log, and your trusted-pro contacts — right on your phone. Everything you add stays on your device.
  • Due and overdue maintenance items are computed on your device from the dates you enter. HomeKeeper does not monitor your home, your systems, or your appliances in any way — there are no sensors, no live monitoring, and no connected devices. It only works with the information you type in.
  • The home report is built on your device from the information you entered, and is shared only when you choose to share it.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your house profile, systems and appliances, maintenance schedule, service log, and contacts are stored in a private database on your phone only.
  • Photos and documents you add to the records vault are copied into the app's private storage on your phone only. They are never uploaded, emailed, or transmitted anywhere.
  • When you delete an item or a record, it is deleted with it. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.

Camera and photos

  • The app uses your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo or document to your records. It never accesses your camera or photos on its own.
  • Nothing about the process leaves your phone.

What the app does not do

  • It does not take payments or ask for bank, card, or account information.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, microphone, or messages.
  • It does not show ads and contains no advertising or tracking software.
  • It does not monitor your home or any system or appliance, and it does not send or receive data from any device.

Children: HomeKeeper is made for adults. It is not directed at children.

Changes: If a future version changes how the app handles information, this policy will be updated and the new version posted at the same address before the change takes effect.
